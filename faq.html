FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Key information for professional buyers considering FPH DREWPOL as a wooden handle supplier.

What wood does FPH DREWPOL use?

The company has confirmed beech wood as its material for the handles presented on this website.

What products do you manufacture?

The stated product focus includes wooden handles for shovels and hammers. Other requirements can be discussed directly.

What is your production capacity?

The maximum production capacity is up to 100,000 pieces per month. This is a maximum capacity figure, not a statement of actual monthly output.

Who do you supply?

The company is interested in working with tool manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors and DIY retail businesses. Please contact the team to discuss your requirements.

Can you manufacture to custom dimensions?

Custom dimensions and specifications have not yet been confirmed. Send your requirements so the team can review them.

What are your minimum order quantities and lead times?

Minimum order quantities and lead times depend on the product and production schedule. Please contact FPH DREWPOL for current information.

Do you hold quality or forestry certifications?

No certification details have been provided for publication. Please ask the company directly for current documentation.

Where is FPH DREWPOL located?

FPH DREWPOL is based in Jodłowa, in the Dębica district of the Subcarpathian Voivodeship, Poland.
